checking helper…

How to start the helper

1. Create a developer app with your broker and put its key in connector/.env (copy .env.example).
2. In a terminal: cd connector then python bullscope_connector.py (or add --demo to try it without a broker account).
3. Click Log in next to your broker, finish the login on the broker's site, come back and click Sync holdings.
Tokens stay in the helper's memory and expire daily. BullScope never sees your password.
